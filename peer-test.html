Drift Atlas

This project lets you explore NOAA drifter records from the North Atlantic in 2024. Choose a place, find the instruments that passed nearby, and follow one of their routes.

Open Drift Atlas ↗

Try it out

  1. Is the project showing live ocean movement or historical records?
  2. Choose a different place and month. How many drifters passed within your search radius?
  3. Follow one drifter. When did it come closest to your place, and which direction did it travel afterward?
  4. What can a single route tell you about the ocean? What would an empty search mean?
